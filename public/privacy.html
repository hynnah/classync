Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Classync is a personal project, not a company or a permanent commercial service. It may be changed, paused, or discontinued at any time.

What we collect

Classync signs you in with Google, so there's no separate password to create or store. When you sign in, we get your name, email address, and avatar from your Google account. We don't touch anything else in your Google account unless you separately turn on Calendar sync (more on that below).

Beyond your Google profile, we store what you put into the app: your Personal to-dos and notes, any Space you create or join, and the tasks and events you or others add to a shared Space.

Personal items stay private

Your Personal to-do list and Notes are yours alone. No Space, and no Organizer of any Space, can see them. A Space only ever sees what's created inside that Space.

What a Space shares

Anything created inside a Space, a task or an event, is visible to every current member of that Space. Notes can't be created inside a Space at all, they're a Personal-only feature. Removing yourself from a Space, or being removed, stops future access. It doesn't erase what already happened while you were a member, since other members' own activity referencing you may still exist.

Google Calendar (optional)

Connecting Google Calendar is a separate, explicit step from signing in. It's never on by default. If you connect it, Classync only asks for calendar event access, not your existing calendar's contents, and stores your refresh token encrypted at rest. Disconnecting revokes that access and removes every event Classync created on your calendar.

Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in, and a second cookie to protect against cross-site request forgery. Neither is used for advertising or tracking across other sites. They're only there to make the app work.

Who else can see your account

Classync has a small number of admin users, typically its creator or maintainer, who can view account and Space status for moderation purposes, for example deactivating an account or a Space. Admin actions are logged. Admins do not see your Personal to-dos or notes.

Where your data is processed, and your rights

Classync is run from the Philippines, and your data is stored and processed there, under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). That law gives you the right to know how your data is used, to access and correct it, to object to certain processing, to have it erased or blocked, to take a copy of your data with you, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) if you think your rights have been violated. You can already view, correct, or delete your own data right from the app. For anything else, use the contact below.

If you're using Classync from outside the Philippines, your information is still sent to, stored, and processed there. If you live somewhere with its own data protection law, for example the EU or UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA, that law may give you extra rights beyond what's written here. Nothing in this policy is meant to take those rights away.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings. This removes your Personal to-dos and notes, and anything you created inside a shared Space, from the live database right away. It can't be undone.

Copies of deleted data may remain for a while in provider managed backups or other disaster recovery systems, since those aren't always removed at the exact same moment as the live data. Those copies are retained and eventually overwritten according to each provider's own backup and retention processes, not a fixed schedule we set ourselves. We don't intentionally keep deleted account data in the live service any longer than necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

If Classync is ever paused or discontinued, we'll handle any data still on file responsibly rather than keep it around indefinitely for no reason.

Age requirement

Classync is meant for users 13 years of age or older, in line with the age confirmation required at sign-in.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
