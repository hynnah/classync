Who can use Classync

You must be 13 years of age or older to use Classync, and you must sign in with a real Google account. Classync has no separate password-based accounts. By checking the age-confirmation box at sign-in, you're confirming you meet this requirement.

Your account

You're responsible for what happens under your account. If you believe your Google account has been compromised, secure it through Google directly. Classync itself has no separate password to reset.

Personal content and Space content

Anything you keep in your Personal to-do list or Notes is private to you. Notes can't be created inside a Space at all, they're a Personal-only feature. Anything you create inside a shared Space, a task or an event, is visible to every current member of that Space, and you're responsible for what you post there.

Don't post anything in a Space you don't have the right to share, or that violates another member's privacy or rights.

Organizers and Spaces

Creating a Space makes you its Organizer, with the ability to rename it, manage membership, and promote or remove other members. Organizers are responsible for how their Space is used. Any Member can leave a Space at any time. If you're the Space's only Organizer and other Members are still in it, you'll need to remove them, promote one of them to Organizer, or wait for them to leave before you can leave yourself, so a Space is never left with Members in it but no one able to manage it. Once you're the only person left, you're free to leave, and the Space is deactivated.

Acceptable use

  • Don't use Classync to harass, impersonate, or share content you don't have the right to share.
  • Don't attempt to access another user's account or another Space you're not a member of.
  • Don't attempt to disrupt or overload the service.

Moderation and account status

Classync's admin users may deactivate an account or a Space that violates these terms. A deactivated account can no longer sign in, and a deactivated Space becomes inaccessible to its members until it's reactivated.

Google Calendar sync

Connecting Google Calendar is optional and separate from signing in. You can disconnect it at any time from Settings, which removes every calendar event Classync created on your behalf.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings. This removes your Personal to-dos and notes, and anything you created inside a shared Space, and it cannot be undone.
