For the things you do in quiet, and the things you build together.

Classync is a shared task and calendar planner for groups. Create a Space for a class, study group, project team, trip, or anything else you’re planning together. Organizers can assign tasks to specific members, while everyone can still keep their own private planner.

01

Everything you need to stay on track

A Space

Create a shared Space for your group and invite others using a six-character code. The person who creates it becomes the Organizer and can manage the Space and assign tasks.

Space tasks

Create tasks for the whole Space or assign them to specific members. Add a category, description, and due date, then see everything on the shared calendar.

My Planner

Keep your own personal tasks separate from the Space. These are only visible to you and stay available even if you are not part of a Space.

02 · One view

See everything on one calendar

Space tasks, Space events, and your personal tasks can all appear on the same calendar. Each one is shown differently so you can quickly tell what belongs to your group and what is just for you.

On privacy

Your planner is yours. A Space only ever sees what a Space is given.

03

How it works

01 Sign in with Google No password to keep. Classync takes your name, email and avatar, and nothing else. Calendar sync is a separate step, later, if you want it.
02 Create, join, or stay solo Create a Space and share its code. Enter someone else's code to join theirs. Or skip both and go straight to My Planner — you can change your mind at any time.
03 Read one calendar Space tasks, Space events, and personal tasks appear on one calendar, while still keeping personal items private.
