01

Three ways to keep time

A Space

Any shared group, not only a class. Name it, share the six-character code, and you become its Organizer — the only person who can assign.

Space tasks

Assigned to named members or left open to everyone. Attachments, categories, and a due date that lands on one shared calendar.

My Planner

Yours alone, always there — whether or not you belong to a Space. No Organizer, in any Space, can see a single line of it.

02 · One view

Everything on one grid, told apart by a stroke

Gold for what a Space asks of you. Blue-grey for events. Warm grey for the private things. Month, week and day are the same calendar, read at three distances.

03

How it works

01 Sign in with Google No password to keep. Classync takes your name, email and avatar, and nothing else. Calendar sync is a separate step, later, if you want it.
02 Create, join, or stay solo Create a Space and share its code. Enter someone else's code to join theirs. Or skip both and go straight to My Planner — you can change your mind at any time.
03 Read one calendar Space tasks, Space events and your own private items sit on the same grid, told apart by colour. Members see what is assigned to them first, then what is open to everyone.
