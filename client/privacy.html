What we collect

Classync signs you in with Google — there's no separate password to create or store. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and avatar from your Google account. Nothing else about your Google account is accessed unless you separately opt in to Calendar sync (below).

Beyond your Google profile, we store what you put into the app: your Personal to-dos and notes, any Space you create or join, and the tasks/events/notes you or others add to a shared Space.

Personal items stay private

Your Personal to-do list and Notes are yours alone — no Space, and no Organizer of any Space, can see them. A Space only ever sees what's created inside that Space.

What a Space shares

Anything created inside a Space — a task, an event, a note tied to that Space — is visible to every current member of that Space. Removing yourself from a Space (or being removed) stops future access; it doesn't retroactively hide what happened while you were a member, since other members' own activity referencing you may still exist.

Google Calendar (optional)

Connecting Google Calendar is a separate, explicit step from signing in — it's never on by default. If you connect it, Classync requests only calendar event access (not your existing calendar's contents), and stores your refresh token encrypted at rest. Disconnecting revokes that access and removes every event Classync created on your calendar.

Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and a second cookie to protect against cross-site request forgery. Neither is used for advertising or tracking across other sites — they exist to make the app work.

Who else can see your account

Classync has a small number of admin users (for a class deployment, typically the instructor or project owner) who can view account and Space status for moderation purposes — for example, deactivating an account or a Space. Admin actions are logged. Admins do not see your Personal to-dos or notes.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings — this removes your Personal to-dos and notes, and anything you created inside a shared Space, right away. This can't be undone.
