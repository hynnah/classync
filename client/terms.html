Who can use Classync

You must be 13 years of age or older to use Classync, and you must sign in with a real Google account — Classync has no separate password-based accounts. By checking the age-confirmation box at sign-in, you're confirming you meet this requirement.

Your account

You're responsible for what happens under your account. If you believe your Google account has been compromised, secure it through Google directly — Classync itself has no separate password to reset.

Personal content and Space content

Anything you keep in your Personal to-do list or Notes is private to you. Anything you create inside a shared Space — a task, event, or note — is visible to every current member of that Space, and you're responsible for what you post there.

Don't post anything in a Space you don't have the right to share, or that violates another member's privacy or rights.

Organizers and Spaces

Creating a Space makes you its Organizer, with the ability to rename it, manage membership, and promote/remove other members. Organizers are responsible for how their Space is used. A Space can be left by any member at any time (an Organizer must first transfer or share Organizer duties if they're the last one, so a Space is never left without anyone able to manage it).

Acceptable use

  • Don't use Classync to harass, impersonate, or share content you don't have the right to share.
  • Don't attempt to access another user's account or another Space you're not a member of.
  • Don't attempt to disrupt or overload the service.

Moderation and account status

Classync's admin users may deactivate an account or a Space that violates these terms. A deactivated account can no longer sign in; a deactivated Space becomes inaccessible to its members until reactivated.

Google Calendar sync

Connecting Google Calendar is optional and separate from signing in. You can disconnect it at any time from Settings, which removes every calendar event Classync created on your behalf.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings. This removes your Personal to-dos and notes, and anything you created inside a shared Space, and cannot be undone.

No warranty

Classync is provided as a class project, "as is," without warranty of any kind. We don't guarantee it will be available, error-free, or suitable for any particular purpose.
